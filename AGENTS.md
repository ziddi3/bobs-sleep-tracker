## Classification

This repository implements **Bob's Sleep Tracker**.

Anything named `Bob's ...` is canonically a tool that gets used, not a place that gets navigated.

Bob's Sleep Tracker may have a website, application, panel, API, storage layer, or container implementation. Those implementation forms do not change its identity as a tool.

It is not Method Hub, Nexus Hub, a business storefront, the Cathedral, or a Cathedral wing.

## Product law

Infrastructure must remain subordinate to the direct wellness-tracking task.

The primary question is: how does the user use Bob's Sleep Tracker to record, understand, and act on sleep information?

Do not turn the tool into a general-purpose hub or navigable world.

Do not deploy over `hub.methodz.ca`.

Do not push unless explicitly authorized.
